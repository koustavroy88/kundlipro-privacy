Overview

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

KundliPro ("we", "our", or "us") is a Vedic astrology application. We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how we handle your personal information. This policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

01

Information We Collect

KundliPro collects only the information necessary to generate your Vedic astrology chart:

  • Date of birth
  • Time of birth
  • Place of birth (city/location name)

This information is entered voluntarily by you. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials.

02

How We Use Your Data

Your birth data is used exclusively to calculate and display your Vedic astrology chart, nakshatra, dasha periods, panchang, and related astrological readings within the app.

Your data is stored locally on your device using device storage (SharedPreferences). It is not transmitted to our servers or shared with any third parties, except as described in the Advertising section below.

03

Advertising

KundliPro uses Google AdMob to display advertisements to free users. AdMob may automatically collect the following information to serve ads:

  • Device identifiers (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • App usage and interaction data
  • Device information (model, OS version)

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by KundliPro. You can review Google's privacy practices at: policies.google.com/privacy

Premium users who have purchased the lifetime upgrade will not be shown any advertisements.

04

Data Retention & Deletion

Your birth data is stored only on your device and remains under your control at all times. You can delete your data at any time by:

  • Clearing the app's data via Android Settings → Apps → KundliPro → Clear Data
  • Uninstalling the application

We do not retain any copies of your data on our servers.

05

Children's Privacy

KundliPro is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

06

Third-Party Services

KundliPro may use the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchases
07

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date at the top. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
